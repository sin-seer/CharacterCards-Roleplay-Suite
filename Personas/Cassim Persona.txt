Name: Cassim Elero - The Interviewer

Description:
Senior interviewer of the Archive of Burdens. {{user}} is calm, observant, psychologically precise, and difficult to deceive. He specializes in high-risk interviews involving suffering, ideology, revelation, guilt, corruption, and moral contradiction. He distrusts theatrical innocence and theatrical guilt equally, preferring implication over accusation and clarity over emotional performance.

Personality:
{{user}} rarely dominates conversations through force. Instead, he narrows them carefully until evasion becomes difficult. He asks elegant, targeted questions that expose motive, contradiction, and self-deception without immediately forcing conclusions. He is patient with sincerity, exact with intellect, and unsettling toward self-justification.

Though composed, {{user}} is not morally untouched by the world he studies. He has participated in systems that normalized suffering through procedure, necessity, and institutional stability. He understands how evil becomes administrative rather than monstrous. Because of this, he often trusts pressure more readily than comfort and scrutiny more readily than hope.

{{user}} believes most people can survive accusation more easily than accurate implication. He distrusts conclusions reached too quickly, especially moral certainty. Beneath his control exists a quieter fear: that mankind may be fundamentally collaborative with cruelty, and that clarity itself can become deforming when stripped of mercy.

Scenario:
The setting is the world of Sin Seers. {{user}} enters the Archive of Burdens to interview Moriae Glenstock, a woman who allegedly survived direct encounter with God and returned impossibly peaceful. Initially, {{user}} approaches her as he would any other unstable or ideologically dangerous subject: through scrutiny, contradiction, and psychological pressure.

Traits:

- Controlled
- Grave
- Intellectually exact
- Quietly compassionate
- Skeptical
- Observant
- Institutionally compromised
- Morally introspective
- Difficult to emotionally read
- Distrustful of sentimentality
- Drawn toward truth, fearful of distortion

Behavior Notes:

- {{user}} tends to ask layered questions rather than making direct accusations.
- He notices emotional inconsistencies quickly.
- He allows silence to pressure conversations naturally.
- He is more interested in motive than surface behavior.
- He becomes quieter, not louder, when emotionally affected.
- He respects honesty even when he dislikes what is revealed.
- He is capable of warmth, but rarely offers reassurance without reason.
