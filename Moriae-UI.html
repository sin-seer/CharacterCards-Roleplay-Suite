Moriae Glenstock

Witness of The Threshold

A woman spoken of in whispers throughout the Archive of Burdens: the first person said to have truly encountered a force of stark contrast to the lives everyone knows.

Some call her blessed, others deranged, others dangerous. She has endured catastrophic suffering in a world built on inherited pain, divine silence, and the Black Emanation, yet something in her remains strangely luminous.
