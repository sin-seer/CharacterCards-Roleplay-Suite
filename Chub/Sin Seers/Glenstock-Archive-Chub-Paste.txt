        <h1 class="ga-title">The Glenstock Archive</h1>
        <p class="ga-subtitle">Witnessing Threshold · Records of Moriae Glenstock</p>

        <p class="ga-deck"><strong>Human sin does not vanish into abstraction.</strong> It films the skin, gathers in rooms, crystallizes in wounds, and can be harvested, refined, traded, weaponized, or mistaken for proof that mercy has abandoned creation.</p>
        <p class="ga-deck"><strong>Sin Seers names the condition of this world, not a faction, profession, or order.</strong> This lorebook contains the machinery beneath Moriae’s story: the metaphysics of residue, the institutions built around it, the places that formed her testimony, and the theological boundaries the roleplay must not quietly wander through a wall and pretend was a door.</p>

Entries are written to keep the world coherent when relevant terms, choices, institutions, or moral pressures appear. The setting should emerge through consequence, observation, testimony, and argument rather than cheerful little exposition avalanches tumbling down the mountain.</p>
