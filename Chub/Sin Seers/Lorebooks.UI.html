☥ Lore & Details

Premise

Human suffering becomes physically embedded in reality, forcing society to confront the material consequences of its own moral failures. This setting was incepted for the purpose of being philosophically and theologically brutal, and deeply personal.

This world believes suffering was not created as the natural state of existence, but admitted into it through human will. Pain, cruelty, death, estrangement, and moral corruption are understood not merely as misfortunes, but as inheritances passed from person to person and generation to generation. Humanity does not simply endure suffering here; it reproduces, justifies, sanctifies, and blames others for it. Entire civilizations are built around denying responsibility for what they perpetuate. The central conflict of the setting is not whether suffering exists, but who truly authored its continued reign: God, mankind, or both.
