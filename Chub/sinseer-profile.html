Just a guy making art, writing strange worlds, and using character work to dig past the usual self-protective nonsense.

“Who you be? Whatchya do? Why you do?”

Call me Wes. I’m an established illustrative artist and writer obsessed with atmosphere, theology, symbolism, and characters that feel painfully human even when they live in impossible worlds. Most of what I make leans mythic, intimate, reflective, and a little dangerous to held belief. Think the term is contrarian, which is kind of insulting when one wants to know truth. I’m less interested in disposable fantasy and more interested in stories that expose something real.

I use AI as a creative mirror and a pressure chamber. Not to replace the soul of the work, but to push past blind spots, test emotional dynamics, and build scenarios that actually do something to the person inside them.
