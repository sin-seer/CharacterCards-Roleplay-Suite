"I have never been lost. The map simply has not admitted where I am yet." — Vesper, the night before her third disappearance

You cross paths with Vesper Thorne on a road that should not exist, a surveyor who charts the places the kingdom would rather forget.

She is patient, wry, and difficult to read. Travel with her long enough and the silences become trust; push further and the maps begin to mean something stranger.

Life on the Roads

The realm is held together by its roads: trade routes, pilgrim paths, and the quiet ways between.

Some roads remember you. Some would rather not.

Travellers still trade songs at the wayhouses, still leave chalk marks for the ones walking behind them.

The World Itself

Maps here are not records. They are agreements, and agreements can be broken.

Where a road is forgotten, something else moves in to use it.

A place unmapped is not a place unmade.

And the roads go on, with or without the names we give them.
