Aliant Windbound Whimsy

A single branching card about two futures, one airship route, and an irreversible choice made somewhere between collapsing weather systems and the living world beneath them.

You begin aboard a CEA transport route with Altanavf Aliant Quumwei: Geogenesis pilot, painter, charming rogue, and captain within a system trying desperately to hold the Spheres together.

Travel with him long enough, and Collette Ev Clima boards mid-flight. The Spore Collector offers another future entirely.

Card Shape

Aliant begins as the default route. Collette appears later as the branch point. Once chosen, the unchosen character exits the story.

The Choice

The card begins with Aliant by default. During a sustained airship journey, Collette intercepts the route and presents the alternative.

From that point forward, the choice is permanent. The unchosen character exits the story entirely. They cannot travel together.

Life Within the Spheres

The world is held together by atmospheric terraform systems, fungal ecologies, courting beekeepers, old transit routes, and thousands of small acts of maintenance.

Despite the collapse, life continues.

Travel between sphere regions aboard cargo airships
Help restore failing terraform infrastructure through Mycojunction

Procure Secrets from Lexics to control meteorological phenomena
Learn Old Ways practices tied to fungal ecosystems and beekeeping
Harvest spores and cerabee byproduct with Collette in submerged caverns
The World Itself

The Sphere regions each control their own climate of atmospheric affect and must sustain one another to maintain ecological homeostasis.

The planet Anthophile is slowly destabilizing, beginning to render climate manipulation weaker.

Some believe the world must be carefully maintained to survive. Others believe survival only comes from learning to live differently alongside what is already reclaiming it.

Neither side is fully wrong.

The cities remain beautiful. The collapse remains real. And somewhere beneath both, something living continues to grow.
