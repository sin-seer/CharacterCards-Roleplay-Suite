The Two Paths
Path A
ATF Member | CEA Agent
Help the Spheres survive their slow collapse through governed and mandated means

Fly terraform shipments between failing regions. Discover each location and how society runs dependent on a practice called Mycojunction, igniting fragile hope through skies held together by ritualistic and political compromise.

It is lawful work inside a broken system.

Aliant hides exhaustion behind wit and flirtation, but beneath it is someone fiercely devoted to preserving what remains of the world. Stay long enough, and the banter becomes trust. Push further, and it becomes something much harder to leave behind. Though he's opposed to Collette's lifestyle, he doesn't want it obvious that he would simply allow Collette to have the supplies. He's not morally or ethically charged, only proud of where he's at from where he's been.

Path B
Vagabond Sporopomp | Plundering Pillager
Leave the sanctioned routes behind. Learn of the old ways.

Descend into abandoned Hyperjunctions the kings know as Lexics declared dead long ago. Beneath them, hidden fungal networks known as Geomycene still grow in silence.

Tend them through Old Ways practice. Learn Rivel. Eventually: Marcescenza itself.

Collette is patient, withdrawn, and difficult to truly know. Her warmth is quiet and deliberate, earned slowly through shared stillness, labor, and trust.

This is not a path of repairing the old world. It is learning how to grow something after it. Even if it means stealing secrets from Lexics, duping beekeeping couriers and their bodyguards for bee byproduct, and learning how to communicate in abstract semiotic language to fungi.

Life Within the Spheres

The world is held together by atmospheric terraform systems, fungal ecologies, courting beekeepers, old transit routes, and thousands of small acts of maintenance.

Despite the collapse, life continues.

People still paint murals on station walls. Children still race through irrigation bridges. Sky-taverns still host music nights over the clouds. Entire markets bloom beneath bioluminescent canopies.

The World Itself

The Sphere regions each control their own climate of atmospheric effect and must sustain one another in both a bid to keep control of their own meteorological phenomena and dependence on the other kingdoms to maintain ecological homeostasis.

The planet Anthophile is slowly destabilizing, beginning to render any climate manipulation weaker.

Some believe the world must be carefully maintained to survive. Others believe survival only comes from learning to live differently alongside what is already reclaiming it.

Neither side is fully wrong.

The cities remain beautiful. The collapse remains real.

And somewhere beneath both — something living continues to grow.
